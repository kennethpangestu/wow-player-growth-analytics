# Decisions log

Every judgment call that could change a result, with the reason. Newest last.

| # | Decision | Why | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Group rows into sweeps when they are less than 3 minutes apart. | Inside a sweep, rows are 5 to 10 seconds apart; between sweeps the gap is 4 to 7 minutes. This gives 137,654 sweeps, within 0.3% of the paper's 138,084 collected snapshots. | `data.SWEEP_GAP_SECONDS` |
| 2 | Keep rows with a garbled race or class and blank only that field. | About 0.1% of rows have digits or stray characters in race or class, but their player, time and level are fine. Dropping them would break sessions. | `data.clean_raw` |
| 3 | Build sessions on collected sweeps only, and allow one missed sweep inside a session. | Thursday maintenance (03:00 to 11:00) and outages remove whole sweeps. Those are not absences. About 1% of back-to-back sightings skip one collected sweep, likely `/who` result limits rather than logouts. | `data.sessionize` |
| 4 | Do not try to match the paper's 667,032 sessions. | The data has 1,979,295 player-days, more than the paper's session count, and no gap tolerance gets close (allowing 6 missed sweeps still gives 2.7M sessions). The paper must define sessions differently. | README, data quality |
| 5 | Weight each sweep by the minutes it stands for, capped at 1.5x the typical interval. | From 2008-11-07 the collector swept about every 15 minutes instead of 10. Counting snapshots x 10 would understate play time by a third after that date. | `data.sweep_table` |
| 6 | Scale DAU on slow-sampling days by the measured capture rate. | Thinning 60 full 10-minute days to 2 of every 3 sweeps keeps about 94% of daily actives. Without the adjustment, post-Wrath DAU would read about 6% low. | `data.thinning_ratio`, `metrics.daily_active` |
| 7 | Flag days with under 50% coverage and leave them out of models and averages. | DAU on days with 30% to 50% coverage averages about 80% of normal, and under 30% less than half. Thursday maintenance days (about 62% coverage) run about 9% below normal and are kept; the day-of-week terms in the launch models absorb the dip. | `metrics.LOW_COVERAGE` |
| 8 | Flag IDs that look shared by two characters and leave them out of avatar-level analyses. | 1,041 IDs drop more than 5 levels between sightings and 465 show two race/class combinations, consistent with a deleted character's name being taken again. They are still online players, so they stay in DAU and MAU. | `data.reused_ids` |
| 9 | Take launch dates from the data (first avatar above the old level cap). | Taiwan launched later than North America: The Burning Crusade on 2007-04-03 (not 2007-01-16) and Wrath of the Lich King on 2008-11-19 (not 2008-11-13). The first Blood Elf, Paladin and Death Knight appear on the same days. | `launch.detect_launch_dates` |
| 10 | Burn-in through March 2006 for growth accounting and new-avatar cohorts. | Every avatar active when collection began looks new in the first months. | `metrics.BURN_IN_END` |
| 11 | New avatars must start at level 10 or below and not be Death Knights. | Higher starting levels mean a transfer or an old character; Death Knights start at 55 and are always alts. | `metrics.new_avatars` |
| 12 | Retention windows instead of single days (D7 = days 7 to 13, D30 = 30 to 59, D90 = 90 to 119). | Exact-day retention on one realm is noisy and sensitive to weekday. | `metrics.RETENTION_WINDOWS` |
| 13 | Launch counterfactual: 84-day pre-period, weekly trend, day of week, Lunar New Year; report 56/112-day and flat 28-day versions too. | Long enough to estimate weekday effects, short enough that the trend is local. The other windows show how much the choice matters. | `launch.counterfactual_lift` |
| 14 | Leave known anomaly days (transfer wave, level-1 wave, plus 3 days) out of launch fits, and keep placebo windows away from them. | The October 2008 level-1 wave raised DAU for a week inside Wrath's pre-period; leaving it in lowers that estimate from +12% to +8% with an interval that includes zero. Both numbers are reported. | `run_analysis.blackout_windows`, `docs/event_calendar.csv` |
| 15 | Guild study timeline: covariates in days 0 to 6, treatment in days 7 to 13, outcome in days 90 to 119; exclude avatars already guilded in week 1 and require activity in week 2. | Covariates must come before treatment, and everyone compared must have had the chance to join in week 2. | `early.guild_study_sample` |
| 16 | Propensity model with log and squared terms; IPW (odds weights) as the main estimate, matching as a check; trim scores outside 0.01 to 0.99. | Hours, sessions and levels are heavily skewed; the linear-only model left standardized differences of about 0.16. With the extra terms every covariate is under 0.02 after weighting. | `early.guild_effect` |
| 17 | At-risk proxy for Q6: fewer active days in the last 14 days than the 14 before. | Placeholder until `ml_churn_scores` exists. | `experiments/population.py` |
| 18 | Primary experiment metric: active days in 28 days, analyzed with CUPED, fixed horizon. | Moves more often than a yes/no retention flag, and pre-period activity predicts it well (correlation 0.66). The peeking simulation shows why the horizon is fixed. | `experiments/design.py` |
| 19 | Sizing baseline: average adjusted DAU of the last 56 days until the teammate's forecast exists; count extra play only from day 30. | Conservative, and it switches to the forecast automatically. | `growth/sizing.py` |
